EPIC attributes the following claim to Hilton: Hilton reported that its AI-boosted segmentation and pricing strategy led to a 5-8% revenue increase alongside a rise in guest satisfaction, as customers received offers more closely aligned to their preferences.

The article states that Hilton uses AI models to refine segmentation and dynamic pricing by analyzing Hilton Honors profiles and booking behaviors, then uses those insights for personalized pricing and promotions in direct channels.
