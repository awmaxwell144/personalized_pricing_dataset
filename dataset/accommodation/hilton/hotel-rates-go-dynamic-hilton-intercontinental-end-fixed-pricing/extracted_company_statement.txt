Greg Cross, Hilton vice president for revenue management and strategies, said Hilton eliminated static consortia pricing in favor of its best available rate, which is dynamic and can change daily and sometimes more than once a day. Cross said that if a static price is not attached to market forces, the price will not be demand-driven, and that clients could pay too much when retail rates dropped below a fixed rate. Cross also said Internet rate transparency made it preferable to have one price available across channels, including consortia, and for that price to be the best available rate.
