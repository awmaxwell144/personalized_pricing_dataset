Ariane Gorin lays out her goals as Expedia Group CEO

By Jamie Biesiada
May 16, 2024
Travel Weekly

After taking over as Expedia Group CEO on May 13, Ariane Gorin laid out her primary goals during the company's Explore conference: international growth and accelerating the company's B2C business.

Gorin said international growth presents a "big opportunity" for Expedia. Regarding the B2C business, recent work on Expedia's technology and brands should come to fruition soon. "This is a year where we'll be able to accelerate thanks to the investments that we've made," Gorin said.

Expedia Group is focused on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo. The brands were brought onto the same platform and share a single loyalty program, One Key. Peter Kern said those projects required merging and unifying millions of traveler accounts, moving from 17 customer relationship management systems to one, and moving from 10 machine-learning platforms to one.

On stage, Gorin said Expedia has a bright future and that AI is an important part of it. She compared AI to the mobile-phone revolution in travel and said Expedia is using AI in many ways, including to personalize travelers' experiences.

For instance, a page layout might look different for one traveler versus another, with images based on what Expedia knows about that traveler. A family looking for spring break travel will see related imagery, helping match the traveler with a product more quickly.

"Some people talk about personalization," Gorin said. "We're actually making it happen."

Gorin said Expedia's AI efforts were enabled by its recent technology transformation. She said AI efforts start with ensuring all data is available and accessible, and that Expedia accomplished that by migrating to a single platform. She encouraged leaders to be thoughtful about infrastructure, data availability, and where to apply AI.
