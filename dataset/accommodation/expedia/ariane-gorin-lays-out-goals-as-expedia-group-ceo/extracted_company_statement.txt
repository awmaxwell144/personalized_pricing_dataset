Gorin said international growth presents a "big opportunity" for Expedia.

"This is a year where we'll be able to accelerate thanks to the investments that we've made," Gorin said.

Peter Kern said Expedia Group is focused on Expedia, Hotels.com and Vrbo; the brands were brought onto the same platform and share One Key. Kern said those projects required merging and unifying millions of traveler accounts, moving from 17 customer relationship management systems to one, and moving from 10 machine-learning platforms to one.

"We stand before you a reimagined Expedia Group, capable of new things, able to move at a pace we never could and innovate faster than anyone in the space," Kern said.

Gorin said Expedia is using AI in many ways, including to personalize travelers' experiences.

"Some people talk about personalization," Gorin said. "We're actually making it happen."

Gorin said Expedia's AI efforts start with ensuring all data is available and accessible, and that Expedia accomplished that by migrating to a single platform.
