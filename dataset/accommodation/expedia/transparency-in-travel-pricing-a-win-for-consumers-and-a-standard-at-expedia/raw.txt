Transparency in Travel Pricing: A Win for Consumers and a Standard at Expedia

Expedia Newsroom
March 10, 2026

Expedia Group said transparency in travel pricing is important for consumers and has long been a standard at Expedia. The company said travelers should be able to compare options clearly and understand the total cost of travel before making a booking decision.

Expedia stated that it does not increase prices based on user data or activity and does not personalize pricing based on any protected personal characteristics. The company said pricing transparency helps travelers make informed choices and supports trust in online travel.

The post emphasized that travel pricing often reflects factors such as supplier rates, taxes, fees, demand, availability, and market conditions. Expedia framed its practices as designed to present clear pricing information and competitive travel options rather than using personal data to raise prices for individual users.

Expedia said it supports transparency and clear disclosure in travel pricing and will continue working with policymakers, partners, and consumers on standards that make pricing easier to understand.
