Orbitz executives confirmed that the company is experimenting with showing different hotel offers to Mac and PC visitors, but said the company is not showing the same room to different users at different prices. They also said users can opt to rank results by price. Orbitz chief scientist Wai Gen Yee said Mac users on average spend $20 to $30 more a night on hotels than PC users and are 40% more likely to book four- or five-star hotels.
