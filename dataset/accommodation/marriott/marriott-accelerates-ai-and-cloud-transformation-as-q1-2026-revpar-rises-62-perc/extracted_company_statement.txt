InfotechLead attributes Marriott's technology strategy to company leadership, stating that during the Q1 2026 earnings call CEO Anthony Capuano highlighted technology modernization as foundational to Marriott's long-term growth strategy.

The article states that Marriott's AI-driven personalization platform uses data from Bonvoy members to deliver customized travel recommendations, targeted promotions, and next-best-action offers.

The article further states that Marriott said advanced AI pricing algorithms are dynamically adjusting room rates and inventory in real time, and that Marriott is using operational AI tools to improve labor scheduling and supply chain logistics.
