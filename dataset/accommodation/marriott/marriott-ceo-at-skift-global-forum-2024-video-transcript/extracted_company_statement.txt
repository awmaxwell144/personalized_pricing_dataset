Skift summarizes Marriott CEO Anthony Capuano's interview by saying he describes the Marriott Bonvoy loyalty program as a central growth engine.

The article states that Marriott wants to shift Bonvoy from a purely transactional platform to one that offers "unique experiences" beyond hotel stays, such as the chance to attend events like the Emmys or Formula 1.

Skift also states that the interview covered Marriott's technological overhaul and recent deals with MGM Resorts and Sonder.
