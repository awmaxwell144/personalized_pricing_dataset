Key Points
Marriott is using generative AI to offer personalized travel experiences.
The company faces challenges with scaling and replacing legacy systems.
Innovations include 'attribute-based booking' and enhanced search functionalities.
Summary
Marriott is transforming itself into a travel retailer by leveraging generative AI to offer personalized guest experiences similar to creating a custom Build-A-Bear. Despite facing significant challenges with scaling and legacy technology, the hotelier is implementing 'attribute-based booking' and automating front desk tasks to enhance customer service. Marriott is also experimenting with new search functionalities to improve user experience and is transitioning to Amadeus IT to support these innovations.
