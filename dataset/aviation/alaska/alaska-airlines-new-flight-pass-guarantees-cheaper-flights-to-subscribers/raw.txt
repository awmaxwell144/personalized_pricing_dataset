Alaska Airlines New ‘Flight Pass’ Guarantees Cheaper Flights to Subscribers
Lock in access to cheaper fares on 100 daily flights between West Coast destinations like Los Angeles, Las Vegas, and Phoenix.
By
Rachel Chang
February 16, 2022
Alaska Airlines
Save Story
Save this story
Save Story
Save this story
Move aside streaming services, there’s a new industry entering the monthly subscription space.
Alaska Airlines
's
Flight Pass
launched today, offering guests a new way to book flights by prepaying for discounted airfare on its California routes, as well as those to nearby Reno,
Phoenix
, and Las Vegas.
By committing to one year of this monthly subscription, passengers will get credits to put toward roundtrip flights on eligible daily routes within the selected west coast region, which includes California airports in Sacramento, Sonoma,
San Francisco
, San Jose, Monterey, Fresno,
San Luis Obispo
, Santa Barbara, Burbank, Los Angeles, Orange County,
Palm Springs
, and San Diego; plus flights to Reno, Las Vegas, and Phoenix.
At the most affordable tier, this plan means guaranteed roundtrip flights for less than $130.
“Flight Pass builds on our mission to offer travelers the most west coast destinations at the best value,” Alaska Airlines’ managing director of business development and products Alex Corey said in a statement. “After two years of staying close to home, guests are ready to travel again and with 100 daily flights from 16 airports… Flight Pass will take them there."
The plan comes in two tiers, with the first distinction being how far in advance tickets must be booked. The most basic one, simply called Flight Pass, starts at $49 a month and requires booking flights 14 days before travel, while the upgraded version—dubbed Flight Pass Pro—starts at $199 a month and allows bookings up to two hours before flight departure time. Both plans allow flight selection as early as 90 days ahead of the travel date.
The next choice comes in the number of roundtrip flight credits per year. The Alaska Airlines Flight Pass plan is $49 for six roundtrip flights annually, $99 for 12 flights, and $189 for 24 flights, while the Flight Pass Pro is $199 for six roundtrip flights, $399 for 12 flights, and $749 for 24 flights.
Flight credits based on the plan will be deposited into the subscriber’s account each month, which they can use to select from eligible flights. Upon booking an itinerary, they will pay an additional fee that’s just $0.01 on most flights, plus required taxes and fees that are typically $14.60 per trip.
Taking everything into consideration, the most basic plan with the minimum of six roundtrip flights adds up to about $763.32 a year, or $127.22 per roundtrip flight. On the high end, the flexible Flight Pass Pro plan with 24 round trip flights comes out to $9,689.28 annually, which breaks down to $403.72 per flight that can be booked last minute. While premium flights may have an additional cost, they’ll still be discounted for members.
The one big caveat is that the booking credits do expire and must be booked for a flight within 90 days before the next set of credits will be deposited. If the credits haven't been used at the time the next credits are added, then they’ll be forfeited, so keeping on top of the bookings is key to get your money's worth.
Besides the booking process, Flight Pass passengers will receive the same class of service Alaska Airlines normally provides, including main cabin seat selection, no change fees, and earned miles on every flight, which can still be redeemed like other miles on the carrier or its
One World alliance partners
. All seats have USB and power outlets, as well as Wi-Fi that’s 20 times faster than normal speeds, allowing access to its in-flight library of more than 500 movies and television shows.
To ensure passenger safety, the airline continues to enact its
Next-Level Care program
, boasting 100 pandemic-era health and safety checks, including a hospital-grade air filters and a partnership with Clorox.
The carrier hopes the new offering will help west coast travelers get back to the skies with increased flexibility and value. As Alaska Airlines' California regional vice president Neil Thwaites, said, “Flight Pass will provide more options, value, and care with every trip that our guests book, while also transforming the travel experience long-term.”
